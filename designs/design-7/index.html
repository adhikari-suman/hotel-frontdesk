Mirror

Bilateral symmetry on a central axis, like the lobby of a grand old hotel

Every screen is built on a visible double-rule axis. The wordmark sits on it, front desk items to its left and admin items to its right, and each screen's one primary action is placed on the axis too.

Arrivals, the front desk and the receptionist live in the green face on the left. Departures and the admin live in the pink face on the right. Check-in and check-out are mirror layouts of each other. Placement mirrors, but text inside rows always reads left to right.
