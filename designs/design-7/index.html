Design 7 of 15 · Alder House front desk

Counting Frame

The front desk read like a soroban. Every one of the 64 rooms is a rod with one bead: pushed up against the beam when a guest is in, half-way when someone arrives today, resting low when the room is free, and a broken red rod when it is out of order. Stays are short rods with a bead per night. The screens themselves sit as beads on one rod across the top, with admin pages past a locked post.
