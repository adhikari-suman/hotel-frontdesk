Receptionist4 people

Runs the desk: bookings, rebooking, cancelling, check-in and check-out, extras, and applying codes guests bring. Sees rooms and codes read-only.

Admin2 people

Sets up the hotel: creates users, adds, edits and deletes rooms, and creates promo codes. Admin-only pages carry a plum tag and are locked for receptionists.

Admins don't take booking actions in this version. Whether they should is an open decision.
