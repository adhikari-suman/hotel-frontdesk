Design 13 of 15, Alder House front desk

Tide Table

The front desk read like a hydrographic chart. Occupancy rises and falls night by night as a tide curve; rooms are soundings on a four-floor chart; every status is a buoy with its own shape as well as its colour. Navigation sits along the bottom as a chart scale bar: desk stations on one side of a datum mark, admin stations on the other, hatched and locked for receptionists. Upright sans for the fixed things (rooms, numbers, controls), hydrographic italic serif for the things that flow (dates, nights, totals, headings).
