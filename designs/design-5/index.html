Reservation Book

Design 5 of 10 · Alder House front desk

The hotel’s bound reservation book, opened flat on navy book cloth. Every screen is a two-page spread with pale-blue ruled lines, a plum margin rule and a plum ribbon that marks today. Thumb-index tabs on the fore-edge are the navigation. Forms are written on the lines, and money sits in the margin like a ledger’s amount column.
