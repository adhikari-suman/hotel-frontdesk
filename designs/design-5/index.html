The idea

The front desk is a set of architect’s drawing sheets. Today is literally a floor plan: four floors, sixteen rooms each, with walls, a corridor and door swings, so staff see where a room is as well as what state it’s in. Stays are drawn as dimension lines across nights. Warnings and blocked actions are redline revision clouds. Each panel is a numbered detail with a callout bubble, and navigation lives where a drawing keeps its index: in a title block on the right edge of every sheet, which also says who is signed in, their role, the date and the sheet number. Desk sheets are A-series; admin sheets are S-series and show a lock to receptionists.
