Design 1 · The Rack

Nine desktop screens for a single-hotel front-desk app, covering the receptionist's day and the admin's setup. Rooms are shown as slips in a room rack, and every record reads like a printed registration card.

Archivo
One family on a width axis: 62% for room numbers and figures, 75% for printed labels, 100% for UI text.
autumn26JetBrains Mono, used only for codes: promo codes and booking references.
ARRDUEOCCVCOOOEvery status carries its front-office code, never colour alone.

Placeholders, not spec

  • 12% tax is a flat placeholder rate used in every total.
  • Extras prices are placeholders: breakfast $18 and dinner $42 per guest per night, arrival pickup $55 per trip.
  • Promo "Offer" field (e.g. 15% off room nights) is not in the brief. It was added so a code carries a value; confirm the discount model.
  • Room prices and counts ($136–$366; 24 / 16 / 16 / 8 rooms), the hotel name and all guest data are synthetic.
