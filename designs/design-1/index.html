Alder House front desk, design 1 of 15

Transit Map

The hotel drawn as a transit diagram. Each room type is a coloured line, and every stay is a journey on that line, from an arrival stop to a departure stop. Availability, rebooking, check-in and the folio all use the same picture: a stretch of line with stops at each end. The navigation is a route map too. Receptionists ride the desk line, and rooms, promo codes and users sit on a dashed admin branch that is locked for anyone without the role. Today's departures are the one loud surface, printed on line yellow.
