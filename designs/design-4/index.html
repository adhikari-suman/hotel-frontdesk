Noughts & Crosses

International Typographic Style on a strict three-module grid: a white field, big flush-left black grotesk and 1 px black rules. The only colour is a lavender field for selection and focus, with taupe as the second neutral for disabled, danger and the admin area.

Room status is drawn as the marks of the game, so the house reads at a glance without colour: O is free, X is taken, and small arrows say who is arriving or leaving.

Placeholders, not spec. The 12% tax, the extras prices (breakfast $18 and dinner $42 per guest per night, pickup $55 per trip), the promo Offer field and the room prices are placeholders to confirm.
