pool lanes

A 1960s motor-lodge-modern front desk, light as a sunny lobby. The day flows down vertical swim lanes, arriving, in the house and leaving, divided by ropes of marigold and white buoys, with the 64 rooms laid out as a pool deck of rounded tiles. One marigold sun marks the single committing action on every screen, and every heading speaks in all-lowercase Jost.
