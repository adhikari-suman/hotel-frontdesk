Alder House front desk · design 3 of 15

Switchboard

The front desk as a hotel telephone switchboard. Every room is a jack in a 16 × 4 field, one row per floor, with a lamp that tells you its state. A booking is a crimson patch cord plugged into a room. The folio is a toll ticket with time-stamped lines. Navigation sits on a key shelf at the bottom of the screen, where the operator's lever keys used to be; admin keys sit behind a lock.
