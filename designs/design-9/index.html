Design 9 of 15 · Alder House front desk

Lit Windows

Stand across the street from a hotel at dusk and you can read it: lit windows are guests in, dark glass is free, a blind half down is someone about to arrive. This direction draws Alder House as an architectural elevation, 4 floors × 16 windows, and uses that façade as the room board everywhere a room appears. Navigation is a lift panel down the right edge; admin floors sit behind a key switch, which is how permissions show up for receptionists.
