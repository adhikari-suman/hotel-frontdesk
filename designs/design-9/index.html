Bedside Clock

The front desk as a bedside alarm clock. Every number is a seven-segment readout with faint ghost 8s behind it, set in backlit LCD windows in a light matte plastic housing. An acid-lime LED marks what's active, and a row of rubber keys along the bottom edge is the navigation.

Nine screens, one committed world: the same housing, windows, segment numerals, annunciator status codes and key row on the receptionist and admin screens alike. Fonts are self-hosted DSEG7 Classic, Chivo and Chivo Mono (all OFL).
