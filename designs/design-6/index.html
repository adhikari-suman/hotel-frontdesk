Night Console

A keyboard-first command console for the 24-hour desk. A command bar across the top is the main way around, and the day is one time-ordered queue of arrivals and departures, split beside whatever item has the cursor. Every action carries its keycap, and hot pink is the cursor: focus, selection and the single committing action on each screen, set on a dark ground made for dim lobby light.

Placeholders, not spec

  • Tax at 12% is a placeholder rate.
  • Extras prices (breakfast $18 per guest per night, dinner $42 per guest per night, pickup $55 per trip) are placeholders.
  • The promo Offer field (15% off, 7th night free, and so on) is not in the brief. It is shown so the totals make sense; it is an open decision.
  • Room prices ($136, $166, $238, $366) are placeholders. All names, refs and bookings are synthetic.
