Alder House front desk · design 6

Mountain & Valley

The front desk as folded paper. Every room is a square sheet, and the fold says what is happening to it: flat means free, a corner folded down means someone arrives or leaves today, fully folded means someone sleeps there, a cut means out of order. Screens are tri-fold sheets split by dashed valley creases, a rebook or edit panel folds over from the right like a flap, and navigation is a row of dog-eared folder tabs folded out of the top edge of the sheet: desk work on the left, the admin office behind a fold line on the right.
