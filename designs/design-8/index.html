Lift Panel

Design 8 of the Alder House front desk. The hotel's lift car panel is the interface. A dark brushed-steel column of round floor buttons navigates the house, a dot-matrix window carries the clock and the tonight tally, and every floor opens as a corridor of eight doors wearing door-hanger status tags. Green means arriving or ready, amber means leaving; the committing action on each screen is a single green-lit pill.
