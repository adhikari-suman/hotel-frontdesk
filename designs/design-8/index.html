Elements

The hotel as its own periodic table. Every room is an element tile: the room number sits where the atomic number would, a two-letter status symbol (Va, Oc, Ar, Dp, Oo) takes the centre, and the guest is the element's name. Navigation is a row of element tiles, with admin screens split off as a separate series, the way the table sets apart its lower block. A booking becomes an enlarged element card whose symbol is the guest's initials, and the folio is written out as a formula. Status is always carried by the symbol text as well as colour.
