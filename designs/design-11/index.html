Warp & Weft

The hotel read as cloth on a loom. Floors and room types run as warp threads, nights run across as weft, and every stay is woven into the grid. Status is carried by weave pattern as well as colour: plain for free, indigo twill for in house, dotted for arriving, striped for departing, basket-hatched for out of order. An indigo selvedge rail holds the navigation, admin threads sit below a stitch line, and working panels rise from the bottom as shuttle trays.
