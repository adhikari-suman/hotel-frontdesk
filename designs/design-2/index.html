The front desk printed as a two-colour risograph job: violet and cyan inks on cool white stock, with every line of text in the deep overprint blue the two make together. Rooms are round key tags hung on a pegboard, one hook-rail per floor, and each status is an ink treatment paired with a front-office code. A horizontal masthead and nav replace the sidebar, and content is set like a broadsheet on a 12-column grid.

Placeholders, not spec

  • 12% tax is a flat placeholder rate used in every total.
  • Extras prices are placeholders: breakfast $18 and dinner $42 per guest per night, arrival pickup $55 per trip.
  • The promo Offer field (for example "15% off room nights") is not in the brief. It was added so a code carries a value. The discount model is an open decision.
  • Room prices and counts (Queen $136, Twin Double $166, Deluxe $238, Family Suite $366; 24/16/16/8 rooms) are placeholders.
