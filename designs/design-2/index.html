The front desk as a station concourse

Arrivals and departures are what a front desk runs on, so this direction borrows the language of the departure board. Every room number, date, reference and total sits on a hinged flap tile with an upper and lower half. The 64 rooms hang on one board, four floors of sixteen. Navigation is a route line: the six desk screens are stops on a solid yellow line, and the three admin screens sit past a dashed zone boundary, locked for receptionists. The palette is deep board navy with a signal-yellow primary action and a small set of status colours that always travel with a short code (ARR, DEP, OCC, FREE, CLN, OFF), so colour is never the only signal.
