Stamp Sheet

The hotel is a sheet of postage stamps. Each of the 64 rooms is a perforated stamp in a 16-across sheet, one row per floor. Prices are engraved denominations, an out-of-order room carries a printed overprint, and a booking is a franked cover whose extras ride along as small stamps. The perforation is the one loud device; everything around it is calm, cool and tabular so the desk can scan it at speed.

Rules the screens follow

Status is never colour alone

Every room stamp carries a word (Ready, Staying, Arrives, Leaves) and hatching marks rooms with a guest in them. Out of order is an overprint.

One primary per screen

Ultramarine is spent on a single action. Destructive actions are carmine outlines, and carmine fill only inside a confirmation.

Permissions are visible

The right-hand album index splits Front desk from Admin behind a lock line. Receptionists see admin tabs locked and promo codes as view only.
