1G-001

Direction

Every screen is an architect's diazo whiteprint: violet linework on white paper, a zoned border, a sheet index for navigation and a title block that says what the sheet is, when it was drawn and by whom.

Rooms are drawn as floor plans and status is a drawing convention that still reads in monochrome, always paired with its code. Stays are dimension lines and tables are set as schedules.

Placeholders, not spec. The 12% tax, the extras prices (breakfast $18 and dinner $42 per guest per night, arrival pickup $55 per trip), the promo code “Offer” field (not in the brief) and the room prices ($136, $166, $238, $366) are placeholders to confirm with the client.
