House Lights

The hotel as a small theatre's box office after the house lights go down. The 64 rooms are seats in a curved four-row auditorium, one row per floor, facing the front desk as the stage. A stay is a ticket with a perforated stub that carries the room number. Today's arrivals and departures run down the left edge as a timed cue list. Navigation sits at the bottom as a row of footlights, and admin-only screens are grouped and marked with a lock.

Dark velvet, not black. Violet is kept for the one primary action on each screen. Every status carries a glyph and a word as well as a colour: ring for free, dot for in house, up triangle for arriving, down triangle for departing, cross with hatching for out of order.
