Leadlight

The front desk as an Arts and Crafts leaded window

Every room at Alder House is a pane of glass set in dark lead came, sixteen panes to a floor, four floors to the window. A pane’s glass tells you its state at a glance: reeded for in house, seeded for arriving, ribbed for departing, clear for vacant, hatched for out of order, always with the word printed on it too. The same leading holds the app together: navigation is a single three-by-three window, the six desk screens in clear glass and the three admin screens in amber glass that stays locked for receptionists. One squared rose marks the single primary action on each screen.
